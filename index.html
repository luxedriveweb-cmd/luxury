What are the requirements for self-drive rentals?

Key requirements include a valid National ID (KTP/Passport), an active Driver's License (SIM A), proof of accommodation, and a refundable security deposit.

Can the vehicle be delivered directly to an Airport or Hotel?

Yes, we provide direct delivery and pick-up services at major airports, five-star hotels, or private residences in supported cities.
